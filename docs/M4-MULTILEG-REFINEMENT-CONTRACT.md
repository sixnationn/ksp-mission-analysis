# Multi-leg n-body refinement gate

Reviewed numerical contract. A `patched_conic_screened_route` is only a date and velocity seed. Its body-centre Lambert endpoints cannot be treated as a physical spacecraft state: starting a propagation there would intersect the departure body. The next numerical stage must accept or construct an explicit spacecraft state at the home parking radius and report every applied impulse in the same inertial frame and SI units as the runtime snapshot.

The first implementation slice is a deterministic full-route **evaluator**, not an optimizer. It takes an exact source hash/frame/epoch, a screened route, explicit launch parking state, four three-component impulse vectors, absolute position/velocity/relative integration tolerances, separate position/velocity/event-time acceptance budgets, and a strict repropagation budget. The impulse and parking checkpoints are at launch, Mars-role arrival, Mars-role departure exactly 5,184,000 SI seconds later, and home return. Venus closest approach is an event root within a stated window around the screened epoch, not a fixed impulse epoch. Propagate the massless spacecraft in the time-dependent field of every imported massive body; apply the initial injection, Mars capture, Mars departure and home capture at the named epochs. During the Mars stay, propagate the captured parking orbit; preserve position continuity and apply only the explicit velocity discontinuities. The Venus encounter is unpowered and continuous. Use actual closest-approach events and imported radii/atmosphere boundaries, not the screen's geometric periapsis, for safety decisions.

An accepted evaluation must meet all of these conditions: source/ephemeris identity and coverage match; at launch, immediately after Mars capture, immediately before Mars departure, and immediately after home capture, the body-relative parking state meets stated tolerances on radius, radial velocity, and tangential speed `sqrt(mu/r)`; each fixed event position and relative velocity meets its stated absolute tolerance; the Mars stay duration is exact, and its pre-departure parking drift stays inside the same explicit tolerances without uncharged stationkeeping; an actual Venus closest-approach root lies in the permitted event-time window, above the source boundary plus safety margin and below the requested maximum encounter radius; no intermediate collision or unsafe event occurs; and exactly four burn magnitudes are separately charged. Repropagate the *same fixed impulses* with tighter spacecraft step/tolerances and a separately reintegrated tighter planetary ephemeris. **Both** runs must pass every named-event, parking and Venus constraint. Compare corresponding event positions in metres, relative velocities in m/s, and event times in seconds against separate disagreement limits; the Venus radius comparison also must fit inside 10% of the positive radial safety margin. Require the ephemeris fit budget, including accumulated velocity fit error over each arc, to consume at most 10% of its position allowance. Bound integration steps and all evaluation counts. A passed result is labeled `independent_nbody_route_evaluated`, not Principia-matched and not optimal.

Observable failure cases before implementation: missing body/atmosphere, stale hash, wrong frame or epoch, nonfinite state or impulse, body-centre launch, a failed parking-radius or circular-velocity condition, excessive Mars parking drift, noncontinuous Mars stay, a distant Venus coast without a true encounter, a powered Venus event disguised as unpowered, unsafe Venus or intermediate encounter, insufficient ephemeris coverage, tolerance budget consumed by ephemeris fit, and coarse/strict disagreement in position, velocity, event time or periapsis radius. A manufactured positive fixture must exercise all five named checkpoints, a genuine close Venus pass and the four burn records; a second fixture must fail a single known margin. The current terminal-position-only refiner does not establish this acceptance. Only after that evaluator passes should a bounded multiple-shooting search vary parking phase, burn vectors and encounter states to satisfy the same checks. Optimizer convergence and smallest sampled delta-v are not global-optimum claims.
