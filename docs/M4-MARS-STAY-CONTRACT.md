# Continuous Mars parking evidence

This is the acceptance gate for changing the fixed-impulse evaluator's Mars stay from **endpoint checked** to **continuously checked**. The interval is the exact 5,184,000 SI seconds from immediately after the Mars capture impulse at `t_a` to immediately before the Mars departure impulse at `t_d`. Evaluate states at their absolute UT in the imported inertial frame, then subtract the Mars centre's position and velocity at that same UT. Use the same snapshot hash, frame, epoch, body `mu` and radius, and source-bound atmosphere altitude as the route evaluation. No impulse or state reset is allowed inside the open interval.

## Pass limits

Let `r = |x_sc - x_Mars|`, `v_r = (x_rel · v_rel)/r`, `v_t = sqrt(|v_rel|² - v_r²)`, `r_0 = R_Mars + parking_altitude_m`, and `v_c = sqrt(mu_Mars/r)`. Use the existing positive request limits `parking_radius_tolerance_m` (`δr`), `parking_radial_velocity_tolerance_mps` (`δv_r`), and `parking_tangential_speed_tolerance_mps` (`δv_t`). For **every** time in `[t_a,t_d]`, require `r > R_Mars + atmosphere_Mars`, `|r-r_0| <= δr`, `|v_r| <= δv_r`, and `|v_t-v_c| <= δv_t`. Capture-after and departure-before are included; positions must be continuous at both burns. The four recorded impulses remain the only velocity discontinuities and the only charged burns.

Find and report every Mars-relative radius minimum (`v_r` changes negative to positive) and maximum (`v_r` changes positive to negative), plus both interval endpoints. Root times are actual UT, not fixed fractions of an integration step. A radius bound between consecutive roots/endpoints must exclude an unobserved excursion; a sign test at finitely many sample points is insufficient because two roots can fall between samples. Similarly, establish conservative interval bounds for `|v_r|` and `|v_t-v_c|`; checking those speeds only at radius extrema is insufficient. Subdivide an unresolved interval until the bounds pass or the declared step/evaluation cap is reached; unresolved is failure, never a pass. Record worst radius, radial-speed and tangential-speed margins and the times/intervals attaining them.

The instantaneous Mars-centred osculating energy `ε = |v_rel|²/2 - mu_Mars/r` is a diagnostic. The speed limits above imply `|ε + mu_Mars/(2r)| <= δv_r²/2 + v_c δv_t + δv_t²/2`; report its maximum and reject nonfinite values. Do not require energy conservation over the stay: other moving bodies perturb the Mars-relative orbit. Energy alone cannot replace the separate radius and speed limits.

## Independent numerical check

Run the identical initial state and four fixed impulses through the existing coarse propagation and a separately integrated tighter planetary ephemeris and spacecraft propagation. Both runs must independently satisfy the continuous bounds. Match each corresponding radius extremum by type and chronology; an unmatched, unresolved, or materially different root fails. Require corresponding root times to differ by at most `disagreement_event_time_s`, radii by at most `disagreement_position_m`, and Mars-relative velocities by at most `disagreement_velocity_mps`. Apply the existing ephemeris fit budget to the full stay, including measured velocity fit error accumulated over 5,184,000 s. Report the maximum observed coarse/strict disagreement and the smallest positive slack to every limit; do not accept a limit whose slack is swallowed by the declared fit/numerical uncertainty. Keep all steps, root refinements, interval subdivisions and evaluations bounded and counted.

## Observable cases before implementation

- Pass: a manufactured captured orbit with an actual 5,184,000 s coast, an apoapsis and periapsis strictly inside the parking shell, no interior burn, and matching coarse/strict extrema. Also run a representative kilometre-per-second parking orbit through the same full duration and declared step cap; a very slow toy orbit alone does not establish usable interval bounds.
- Fail: an interior apoapsis exceeds `r_0 + δr` while both burn-side endpoints pass; separately, an interior periapsis falls below `r_0 - δr` or the imported atmosphere boundary.
- Fail: radius stays in range but interior radial speed or tangential speed leaves its limit; an energy-only check must not admit it.
- Fail: a pair of extrema lies between ordinary event samples, a root is unbracketed, or interval subdivision reaches its cap. A coarse pass with a strict failure or an unmatched root also fails.
- Fail: a hidden stationkeeping impulse, a position jump at capture/departure, wrong Mars body/frame/epoch/source atmosphere, nonfinite state, or an ephemeris that does not cover the full stay.

Passing this gate supports only a **numerically checked, fixed-impulse Mars parking stay in the imported n-body approximation**, at the stated tolerance and convergence budgets. Coarse/strict agreement is evidence, not a rigorous bound on all model error. It does not validate the screened Lambert legs, find an optimal route, establish a surface stay, or establish agreement with installed KSP/Principia. Until the interval and velocity bounds above exist, keep `mars_stay_continuously_verified=false` and the checkpoint-only result label.
